# Code of conduct

## Our community

The `helm-charts` community welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help keep them
respectful, inclusive, and focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and use welcoming language.
- Discuss ideas and technical choices without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help others learn.
- Respect different perspectives, accessibility needs, and personal boundaries.
- Protect other people's privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks,
and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.
Repeated disruption and unwelcome contact are also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
project communication spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project
in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer at
[anatolek@ukr.net](mailto:anatolek@ukr.net). Include the relevant links, what happened,
and any context that can help assess the situation. Do not post sensitive personal
information in a public issue.

Reports should be handled with discretion. Information should be shared only as
needed to investigate and address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit inappropriate content and ask participants to
correct their behavior. Depending on the severity and repetition of the conduct,
responses may include a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from
project spaces. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate restrictions.

Participants may use the same private contact to provide additional context or ask
for reconsideration of an enforcement decision.
